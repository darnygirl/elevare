## Pricing — INTEGRITY RULE

Annual contracts ONLY where delivery is a defined, finite product:

```
✗ NO  General individual annual    — open-ended access, can't promise 12mo
✗ NO  Pro individual annual        — open-ended access, can't promise 12mo
✓ YES Pro CORPORATE cohort annual  — bounded cohort × N seats × contract
✓ YES Med 12-week cohort           — already cohort-shaped, defined outcome
✓ YES OET Exam Bundle ($449)       — already one-time, defined deliverable
```

**Why:** we sell what we can deliver. Selling 12 months of unspecified value
to an individual creates an obligation we'd hustle to fulfill instead of
building. Monthly subs let people leave anytime — that's honest. Corporate
cohorts have defined start/end/outcome — that's a real product.

## Cold-start playbook (no warm list, $0 ads)

**Reality:** No reachable former students (Preply blocked contacts).
No active network (loner, years offline). Starting from zero on the
audience side. Strategy must respect this.

**Unfair advantage:** Nurse + TESOL combo. Most OET coaches are pure
English teachers; most nursing communicators don't teach English.
Debby is both. Med tier exploits this directly.

**Tier priority:** Med tier first. Why:

```
Med    $699/mo   one client = covers full move + buffer
       Concentrated ICP — nurses prepping OET have specific forums
       Defined pain — real exam date, real urgency
       Nursing background = instant credibility
       FASTEST path to cash from cold start

Pro    $299/mo   slow corporate cycle (4-8 weeks) — too slow for Day 18
General $99/mo   too diffuse, no concentrated community for cold start
```

**The community-first ladder (lowest → highest avoidance):**

```
WEEK 1   MAP + LURK     find 3-5 communities, read posts, take notes
WEEK 2   HELP PUBLICLY  answer 2-3 questions/day, no pitch, sign as you
WK 2.5   COLD-WARM DMs  DM people who've already seen you help others
WEEK 3+  CLOSE + REPEAT first Med signups → testimonials → louder
```

**Why this beats cold DMs from Day 1:**
- Reading is zero-exposure (Week 1)
- Public helping is medium-exposure (Week 2) — no rejection possible
- DMs come AFTER they've seen you be useful (Week 2.5) — not strangers
- Lower avoidance = higher consistency = compounds

**Communities to map (starting points):**

```

  - "OET Preparation"
  - "OET Exam Preparation"
  - "Nurses Migration to [UK / AU / Canada / etc]"
  - "Filipino Nurses Abroad" (or regional equivalent)

REDDIT
  - r/OET (small, focused)
  - r/Nursing (large, broad)
  - r/IMG (international medical graduates)

TELEGRAM
  - search "OET prep", "nurse migration"

DISCORD
  - disboard.org search "OET" / "nursing students"
```

Goal Day 1: join 3 communities, read 10 posts each.
Goal Day 7: have answered 10+ questions across them.
Goal Day 14: 5-10 saved profiles ready to DM with context.

FOR TUTORS · BETA

Run your first Elevare class

You already know how to teach. This is just where the class happens. Five minutes to read, then you're ready.

1

Open the classroom

Go to elevaremind.io/frontend/live-classroom.html and click the "Host as Tutor" tab. No login, no install — it runs in any browser (works great on phone too).

2

Create your room

Pick a session type — it generates a room name like elevare-med-101. You can rename it to anything memorable. This is your private classroom for the session.

3

Share the link with your students

Tap "Share on WhatsApp" — it writes the message for you with the join link. Send it to your students (the same ones you already teach on Zoom/Meet). They click and they're in. No account, no payment.

💡 Send the link 10 minutes before class so everyone arrives on time. Pin it in your group chat.
4

Teach like you always do

You have HD video, screen-share, and chat — everything Zoom gives you. Teach your normal . The only difference: your students are now experiencing Elevare.

5

Ask for 2 minutes of feedback

At the end, send your students the feedback link: elevaremind.io/frontend/beta-feedback.html — or just tell them to tap "Share session feedback" on the classroom page. This is the whole point of the beta: we learn what to fix.

What we're trying to learn

Three things from this beta — keep them in mind:

  • Does the tech work? Could everyone join, hear, and see clearly?
  • Is it valuable? Did students leave feeling more confident?
  • Would you use it again? Honestly — would you run your classes here instead of Zoom?
🔒 A note on privacy: beta rooms are open to anyone with the name, so use a unique room name each session and don't reuse old ones. Password-protected rooms are coming.

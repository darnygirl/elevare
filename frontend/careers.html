Contract Remote Per-cohort Healthcare

OET Lead Instructor

Lead delivery of our 12-week OET preparation programme for a corporate hospital cohort (first cohort: June 2026, Bangkok). Cohorts are capped at 10 to preserve individualised feedback. Reports to founder; works alongside our consulting clinical SME.

What you'll do

  • Deliver 2 × 90-minute live group sessions per week (online, evening Bangkok time)
  • Provide one individualised written or spoken audit per participant per week
  • Administer 3 full mock tests under exam conditions (Weeks 4, 8, 11) with structured analysis
  • Track individual progress against OET sub-test benchmarks; flag plateaus early
  • Coordinate weekly with founder and clinical SME on curriculum and cohort progress

Required

  • Recognised TESOL / CELTA / Trinity / TEFL certification (120hr+, named issuer)
  • Native or near-native English proficiency
  • Demonstrated experience teaching adult learners in professional contexts
  • Reliable internet, professional video setup, quiet delivery environment
  • Available evenings (Bangkok time, UTC+7) for cohort sessions

Strongly preferred

  • OET-specific experience — teaching, examining, or test-taking
  • Healthcare background — nurse, doctor, midwife, paramedic, or allied health
  • Track record with measurable outcomes (pass rates, score improvements) you can speak to

Pay & structure

  • Per-cohort flat fee, USD via Wise (full figure shared at first interview)
  • Payment milestones: kickoff (Week 1), midpoint (Week 6), completion (Week 12)
  • Performance bonus possible on cohort pass rate (Grade B threshold)
  • Path to ongoing engagement across subsequent cohorts based on mutual fit

Application

  • Email careers@elevaremind.io with subject line OET Lead Instructor — [Your Name]
  • Include CV, brief cover letter, optional teaching artefact (5–10 min video, lesson plan, or feedback sample), and 2 professional references
  • Process: clinical-fit interview (30 min) → pedagogy interview with founder (45 min) → reference check → offer within 5 business days. ~2 weeks application to offer.

How we work

  1. We sell what we can deliver. No hype. The promise on the website is the experience in the cohort.
  2. The student's outcome is the metric — exam pass, fluency milestone, confidence gain. Not engagement vanity, not session counts.
  3. Clinical and professional context matters. Generic English coaching does not pass OET, and does not move careers.
  4. Cohorts are capped (10 for OET, 4–8 for General groups) to preserve individualised feedback. We don't scale by diluting.
  5. Recruitment is on credentials, capability, and integrity — not nationality, age, or background.

No role open that fits?

We're early. New roles open as cohorts grow — facilitators for General groups, curriculum collaborators, B2B partnership leads. If your background fits the work above, write a short note. We read everything; we reply when there's something real to discuss.

## About Elevare

Elevare is a structured language coaching practice for working professionals globally. We are launching a 12-week OET preparation programme designed specifically for nursing and allied healthcare staff preparing for OET Grade B (350+).

Our first corporate client is a major Chiang Mai hospital. We expect to run multiple concurrent cohorts as the programme scales, with our long-term vision being a globally-deployable methodology serving healthcare professionals across markets.

Our principles:
- We sell what we can deliver — honesty over hype.
- The student's exam outcome is the metric, not engagement vanity.
- Clinical context matters. Generic English coaching does not pass OET.
- Cohorts are capped at 10 to preserve individualised feedback.

---

## What you'll do

```
Cohort delivery (per 12-week cohort)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Deliver 2 × 90-minute live group sessions per week
  (online, evening Bangkok / Chiang Mai time to accommodate
   nursing shift schedules)
- Provide one individualised written or spoken audit per
  participant per week (~10 audits/week at full cohort)
- Administer 3 full mock tests under exam conditions
  (Weeks 4, 8, 11) and produce structured analysis for each
- Track individual participant progress against OET sub-test
  benchmarks; flag plateaus and learning blockers
- Coordinate weekly with Elevare founder on curriculum delivery
  and cohort-wide progress
- Coordinate with Sarah (clinical SME) for scenario validation
  and Q&A sessions on clinical-context content
- Produce a final cohort report at Week 12 documenting
  participant outcomes and recommendations
```

## Required qualifications

```
- Recognised TESOL / CELTA / Trinity / TEFL certification
  (minimum 120 hours from a recognised issuer — please name
   the issuer in your application)
- Native or near-native English proficiency
- Demonstrated experience teaching adult learners in
  professional contexts (corporate, university, or B2B)
- Comfort with online cohort delivery (group sessions plus
  individual feedback cycles)
- Reliable internet, professional video setup, quiet
  delivery environment
- Available evenings (Bangkok time, UTC+7) for cohort
  sessions — typically two evenings per week
```

---

## Strongly preferred

```
- OET-specific experience (teaching OET prep, OET examiner
  experience, or OET test-taker experience yourself)
- Healthcare background — currently or previously practicing
  nurse, doctor, midwife, paramedic, or allied health
  professional
- Experience with non-native English speakers in clinical
  or healthcare contexts
- Track record with measurable outcomes (pass rates, score
  improvements) you can speak to
- Curriculum design experience or willingness to refine
  Elevare's existing curriculum based on cohort observations
```

---

## What success looks like

```
- 80%+ of cohort participants achieve OET Grade B or higher
  on the cohort-completion mock test
- Participant satisfaction score of 4.5/5 or higher on
  end-of-cohort survey
- All weekly audits delivered within 48 hours of submission
- All mock test analyses delivered within 7 days of test date
- Constructive contribution to curriculum refinement between
  cohorts
```

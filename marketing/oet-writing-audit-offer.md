## Delivery process — repeatable, 1.5-2 hr per audit

```
STAGE 1   INTAKE (5 min)
  Student sends letter via email or shared doc.
  You confirm receipt + send: "I'll have your audit back within 48 hours."

STAGE 2   READ + DIAGNOSE (20-30 min)
  Read letter once for sense.
  Read again with OET grader's checklist:
    - Purpose: is the request/handover clear?
    - Content: what's prioritized? what's missing? what's irrelevant?
    - Clarity: would a busy clinician understand this in 30 sec?
    - Language: register, accuracy, tone
  Mark up 3-5 specific fixes (not 20 — they'll drown).

STAGE 3   RECORD VIDEO (20-30 min)
  Loom or Zoom-self-record. Show the letter on screen, talk through:
    - What they did well (1 sentence — fast)
    - The 3 biggest fixes, in order of impact
    - Why each matters from a clinical-grader viewpoint
    - One specific drill they can do this week to embed the fix
  End with: "If you want weekly feedback like this + speaking practice,
  the 12-week Med programme is $699/mo. Reply if interested. Otherwise,
  no pressure — go pass that exam."

STAGE 4   DELIVER (10 min)
  Email: video link + annotated letter (PDF or commented Google Doc).
  Subject: "Your OET Writing Audit — [Name]"
  Body: 2 sentences. "Here's your audit. Watch the video first, then
  read the marked-up letter. Reply with any questions."

STAGE 5   FOLLOW-UP (5 min, 7 days later)
  Single email: "How did the next letter go? Anything still tripping you?"
  Either: they reply with a question (potential Med upsell) OR they say
  thanks (you ask for testimonial) OR no response (move on).
```

**Total per audit: ~1.5 hr.** First few will take longer; speed comes after #3-4.

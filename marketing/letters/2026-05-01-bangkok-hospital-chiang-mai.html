Programme Objectives

By the end of the 12-week programme, participating nurses will:

  1. Achieve OET Grade B (350+) across all four sub-tests — Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking — measured against current OET scoring rubrics.
  2. Produce clinically-prioritised written communication — referral letters and discharge summaries that meet OET grading standards for purpose, clarity, content selection, and professional register.
  3. Demonstrate professional spoken communication in nurse-patient and nurse-colleague roleplay scenarios, applying appropriate empathy markers, clinical vocabulary, and OET-aligned interaction patterns.
  4. Complete OET Listening and Reading sub-tests within standardised timing, applying targeted comprehension and inference strategies.
  5. Apply diagnostic strategies for common Grade C plateaus — identifying recurring grammatical, structural, and clinical-relevance errors that prevent score progression.
  6. Build sustainable test-day readiness — reducing exam anxiety through repeated mock-test exposure and individualised feedback cycles.

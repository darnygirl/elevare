### About Elevare

Elevare is a structured language coaching practice serving working professionals globally. The OET preparation programme is delivered by a dedicated team: a lead instructor recruited specifically for clinical-context language coaching, supported by subject-matter advisory from practicing healthcare professionals — including a consulting surgeon who contributes scenario validation and live Q&A. The programme is founded and architected by Deborah Madtrwoth, whose background in nursing and TESOL-aligned language coaching informs a curriculum that bridges clinical reasoning with OET-graded communication standards.

Programme delivery team is finalising in May 2026 to ensure all instructors meet the clinical-context coaching standards required for OET preparation. Final team composition will be confirmed in the formal proposal.

Elevare's methodology recognises that OET is fundamentally a test of professional communication — not abstract English proficiency — and our delivery is designed accordingly.

---

### Programme objectives

By the end of the 12-week programme, participating nurses will:

1. **Achieve OET Grade B (350+) across all four sub-tests** — Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking — measured against current OET scoring rubrics.
2. **Produce clinically-prioritised written communication** — referral letters and discharge summaries that meet OET grading standards for purpose, clarity, content selection, and professional register.
3. **Demonstrate professional spoken communication** in nurse-patient and nurse-colleague roleplay scenarios, applying appropriate empathy markers, clinical vocabulary, and OET-aligned interaction patterns.
4. **Complete OET Listening and Reading sub-tests within standardised timing**, applying targeted comprehension and inference strategies.
5. **Apply diagnostic strategies for common Grade C plateaus** — identifying recurring grammatical, structural, and clinical-relevance errors that prevent score progression.
6. **Build sustainable test-day readiness** — reducing exam anxiety through repeated mock-test exposure and individualised feedback cycles.

---

### Programme structure (12-week overview)

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Diagnostic & Foundation | 1–2 | Baseline assessment, OET format orientation, individual learning plans |
| Listening & Reading | 3–4 | Clinical-context comprehension, timing strategy, vocabulary in healthcare contexts |
| Mid-cohort Mock Test 1 | 4 | Full timed mock + individual performance analysis (calibration) |
| Writing | 5–7 | Referral letters, discharge summaries, content selection, professional register |
| Mid-cohort Mock Test 2 | 8 | Full timed mock + writing-focused analysis (validates progress) |
| Speaking | 9–10 | Nurse-patient and nurse-colleague roleplay, empathy phrases, fluency |
| Final Mock & Refinement | 11 | Full mock under exam conditions + targeted weak-area drills |
| Exam Readiness | 12 | Final exam-day preparation, registration support, confidence drills |

**Weekly delivery format (proposed):**
- Two × 90-minute live group sessions (online, evening Bangkok time, accommodating shift schedules)
- Asynchronous practice materials and self-paced drills (accessible 24/7)
- One individualised writing or speaking audit per week per participant
- Three full mock tests across the programme (Weeks 4, 8, and 11)
- Cohort-wide progress tracking with periodic reports to Bangkok Hospital coordinator

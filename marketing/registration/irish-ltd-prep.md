## Process (when ready to execute)

```
DAY 0 (before filing)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Check company name availability
   → cro.ie → Search → Company Name
   → If "Elevare Mind Limited" taken, try alternatives

2. Pick formation service (recommend companyformations.ie
   €99 package or equivalent)

3. Gather all info above into a single doc shared with Donal

4. Donal + Debby both review + sign required consent forms
   (formation service emails templates)

DAY 1 (filing)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
5. Submit application via formation service
   ─ Pay €99-200 (formation service fee)
   ─ Service files Form A1 + Constitution with CRO

6. CRO processing: 1-3 business days for online filing

DAY 1-3 (waiting)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
7. Receive Certificate of Incorporation (PDF)
   ─ Includes Company Number (CRO format: e.g. 567890)
   ─ Date of incorporation

DAY 3-5 (post-incorporation)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
8. RBO filing (mandatory, within 5 months but do immediately)
   ─ rbo.gov.ie → file beneficial owners
   ─ Free if done online
   ─ ⚠️ If Debby has no PPS or Tax ID, apply for VIN first
      (free, takes 1-2 days via cro.ie)

9. Open business bank account (Wise Business or Revolut Business)
   ─ Use Certificate of Incorporation
   ─ Link to deborah@elevaremind.io

10. Update Stripe / payment processor with company details

11. Update site footer + legal pages with:
    "Elevare Mind Limited
     Company No. [CRO number]
     Registered in Ireland"
```
